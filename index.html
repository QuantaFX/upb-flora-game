The Cordillera Digital Herbarium

The mountain mist is lifting over UP Baguio as the campus celebrates Native Plants Week! However, the Department of Biology’s Digital Herbarium Exhibit is still missing 10 native & highland plant records.

Prof. Racquel Barcelo has called on you to explore the campus terraces, document each plant’s traditional & scientific uses, and build a photographic record with students and faculty!

🥾
1. Climb the 4 Campus Terraces Use WASD / Arrows or the mobile D-Pad to walk, and hold Shift / B to run across stairs from the Oblation Plaza up to the Summit Quad.
💬
2. Follow Quest Bubbles (? & !) Approach mentors with floating bubbles (start with Prof. Barcelo near the Oblation!) and press E / Space / A to accept missions.
🐾
3. Meet Campus Life Chat with roaming students and pet the friendly campus cats, dogs, and birds (E / A) along the walkways!
🔍
Step 1 · OBSERVE Clues First Examine sparkling quest plants (✨) or rustling tall grass. Choose 🔍 OBSERVE to inspect Leaves, Flowers, Aroma, and Habitat.
🏷️
Step 2 · IDENTIFY the Species Match the clues to the right plant in 🏷️ IDENTIFY before your 3 Field Focus (❤️❤️❤️) hearts run out!
🌿
Step 3 · COLLECT or PHOTOGRAPH Gather a sustainable sample (🌿 COLLECT) or take a conservation photo (📸 PHOTOGRAPH), then review all 15 species in your Herbarium (J)!
